# System audit, Nov 10 2026

Everything here is the Alpaca **paper** account. Nothing in the repository can place a real-money order
(`vpa_trading/live.py` only talks to `paper-api.alpaca.markets`).
The version before this audit is commit `9101edc` (local tag `pre-audit-2026-11-10`).

## 1. The three plans, as the code runs them

| | Candle scanner (`vpa-`) | Alert mode (`alert-`) | TSLA breakout (`nb-`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Fade a rejection candle at the top / bottom of a move, at a 1h / 4h / daily fair value gap | Your by-eye setup: pivot wick + volume surge + stretch + oscillators | Trend day: price leaves its usual range for that time of day |
| Stocks | Mag 7 | Mag 7 | TSLA |
| Candles | 9m, 15m | 5m, 9m, 15m | 1m data, decisions every 30 min |
| Entry | Stop order 1 cent past the candle, valid 2 candles, cancelled if the wick goes first. Longs need volume 3x usual | Same order type. Volume 2x, wick 0.5 ATR past the 15m EMA 9, all three oscillators 75+ / 25- | Market order at 10:00, 10:30 ... 15:30 ET when price is outside the band |
| Exit | Half at +0.5R then breakeven, rest at 1R; stop past the wick | Same | Thirds at +1% / +2%; half off on the 15m oscillator at 80/20; breakeven after the first trim; opposite pivot candle on 3x volume; back inside band / VWAP |
| Size | $1,000 risk, max $100k of stock, one add allowed | $500 risk, max $100k | $75k of stock, 2.5% safety stop |
| Hours | Entries 10:00-15:30 ET, flat 15:50 | Same | Same |
| Mode | Paper live + replay | Paper live + replay | Paper live + replay + 5-year backtest |

Contradictions between the plan and the code:

- **"$1,000 risk per trade" is rarely what happens.** The $100k cap decides the size on almost every trade. Across the
  60 signals logged so far the median real risk was $381 (range $96 to $1,000).
- **The module notes still described the first version** (target +0.3%, 5m candles) in places; the live settings come
  from the launch command.

## 2. Honest state of the edge

| Strategy | 5-year test (after costs) | Paper so far (Nov 4-9) |
|---|---|---|
| Candle scanner | **Loses**: -0.09R per trade before Nov 2024, -0.08R after (29,441 trades, `reports/trim_test.md`) | 18 trades, 50% won, -$39 |
| Alert mode | Did not beat costs (`reports/osc_pivot.md`) | 1 trade, -$372 |
| TSLA breakout | +9.1 bps/day before Nov 2024, +3.5 after (Sharpe 0.56) | 5 trades, 4 won, +$2,344 |

- The candle scanner has no measured edge. It is a log of your setups, not a tested strategy. Half-at-0.5R raises the share
  of winning trades (49% to 65%) without changing the average.
- The breakout is the only part with a positive test, and it is weak. TSLA was the best of 20 tickers tried, which
  flatters it. Since Nov 2024 the result is about 0.8 standard errors from zero; the Mag 7 group as a whole is slightly
  negative over that period. Yearly results fell from +21 bps/day (2021) to about zero (2024-2026).
- 24 paper trades prove nothing either way (+$1,933 in total, t-statistic 0.7).

## 3. Findings

### High

| # | What was wrong | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | The half-at-0.5R split cancels the bracket's stop and target, then places two new exit orders. If the cancel took more than 1.5 s, or the scanner was restarted in between (it restarts every 2 hours), the old code never came back to it | The position was left with **no stop and no target** until the 15:50 flatten | Fixed. The next minute finishes the job, also after a restart. Exit orders are capped at the shares still held. 5 tests |
| H2 | Flatten time 15:50 ET and last entry 15:30 were hard-coded | **Nov 27 and Dec 24 close at 13:00.** Positions would have been carried over the close (Nov 27: over the weekend) | Fixed. Times come from Alpaca's calendar: last entry 30 min before the close, flat 10 min before. 2 tests |
| H3 | Any network error or bad reply inside the minute loop ended the scanner | Open trades lose their management (half exits, breakout exits) until the next hourly check | Fixed. A failed minute is logged and the loop continues; the flatten retries |
| H4 | The breakout only checked the candle scanner's orders before entering. It ignored alert-mode trades and positions from before a restart, and ignored everything when reversing | Alpaca keeps one net position per stock. A breakout buy against a scanner short cancels both out and leaves stray stop orders that can open a new position | Fixed. It reads the shares other strategies hold from Alpaca before entering; on a reversal it only exits. 2 tests |
| H5 | No daily loss limit | Worst case if everything stops out together: 7 stocks x ($1,000 + $1,000 add) + 7 x $500 alerts + $1,875 breakout = about $19,000, 19% of the account. The Mag 7 move together, so same-direction losses cluster (Nov 5: 3 scanner trades, 3 losses) | Built as `--max-daily-loss`, **off** until you choose a number (question 1) |
| H6 | The candle scanner loses money in the 5-year test | See section 2 | Not a code fix (question 3) |
| H7 | Real risk per trade is set by the $100k cap, not by $1,000 | Results in "R" and in dollars do not line up; a tight stop risks $100, a wide one $1,000 | Not changed (question 2) |

### Medium

| # | What was wrong | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Live signals use the free IEX feed (2-3% of all trades); tests use the full tape | On Nov 6 and 9 the full tape gave 10 scanner signals, the live feed 6 (all 6 were among the 10) | Needs the paid data plan (question 4) |
| M2 | Tests assume about 2 bps of cost per round trip | Paper entries alone slipped 2.8 bps on average (0.08R; 17 fills). The real scanner result is likely worse than the tested -0.08R | Not changed. Assumption, not measured: exits slip similarly |
| M3 | A replay loaded the breakout state file left by an earlier replay of the same day | Two replays of Nov 5 gave different breakout trades | Fixed. Replays start clean; two runs now match exactly |
| M4 | Half days were not skipped when building the breakout's bands (gaps were filled before counting bars) | A half day pulled the 16:00 band value from 3.89% to 3.76% in the test case | Fixed. 1 test |
| M5 | Replay and the candle backtests cancelled a trade when one 1m bar both triggered the entry and took the wick | Counting those as filled-then-stopped adds 37 trades to 29,441 and moves the average by -0.001R: real but tiny | Fixed in replay; `STRICT_SAME_BAR=1` in `trim_test.py` |
| M6 | No check that price data is fresh | A stalled feed would let the breakout decide on an old price | Fixed. Breakout check and new signals are skipped when bars are over 3 min old; the loop waits for the last minute's bar. 1 test |
| M7 | Exit orders could be placed for shares already closed (after a flatten), which opens a new position | Found while writing H1's tests | Fixed. 2 tests |
| M8 | Posted levels never expired | The Nov 9 levels would still tag signals in December | Fixed. Ignored after 7 days. 1 test |
| M9 | No tests at all | - | 30 tests, `python -m pytest tests`, with an in-memory stand-in for Alpaca |

### Low

- `eod_report.py` printed today's account equity on reports for past days. Fixed.
- The backtests took "prior close" from the last full session. Fixed in the code; it changes **no day** in the 5 years of
  TSLA data, so no result moves.
- The breakout backtest holds to 16:00, live flattens at 15:50, and live takes the open and prior close from IEX. Not
  measured, not changed.
- Earnings, Fed and CPI days are not marked. All trades are flat by the close, so there is no overnight gap risk, but
  volume and range rules behave differently on those days.
- Paper fills are kinder than real ones: no queue, no market impact, and stops fill during fast moves. Halts and
  short-sale restrictions are not modelled anywhere.
- The 13:52 MST backstop flatten is three hours late on a half day. The scanner now flattens itself at 12:50 ET, but only
  if it is running (question 5).
- The TerraShade card reader has not seen a real card yet (first check today 8:20 MST).
- A breakout safety stop that fills in pieces can be logged as a profit level. Rare; not changed.

## 5. Open questions

1. **Daily loss stop.** Turn it on? I suggest $3,000 (about 3% of the account, three full-size losers).
2. **Sizing.** Keep the $100k cap (real risk $100-$1,000, median about $380), or make the risk truly fixed?
   Fixed $1,000 needs positions up to about $1M on tight stops; the account's buying power is $407k.
3. **Candle scanner.** It tests negative. Keep it at $1,000 as a record of your setups, or cut it (say $250) until it has
   100+ trades to judge?
4. **Data.** The full-tape feed (Alpaca Algo Trader Plus, about $99 a month) would make live signals match the tests.
   Worth it only if you plan to act on these signals.
5. **Half days.** Add a backstop flatten at 10:52 MST for Nov 27 and Dec 24?
6. **Very tight stops.** A quarter of signals had a stop under 21 bps, where costs of 2-3 bps are over 10% of the risk.
   Skip signals with a stop under, say, 15 bps?
